Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 2.0

Notice: LPSG Fetcher operates on a privacy-first principle: no analytics trackers, no advertisements, and no harvesting or sale of personal data.

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

This Privacy Policy describes how LPSG Fetcher ("we", "us", or "our") processes information when you use our web workspace. Data processing happens in your browser whenever possible, and your session credentials remain under your direct control.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not set marketing, profiling, or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No Personal Profiles: We do not construct user behavioral profiles, track your browsing history, or monitor searches.
  • No Telemetry / Analytics: No third-party analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixels) are embedded in this application.

3. How Session Cookies Are Handled

When you upload your cookies.txt session export:

  • Client-Side Storage: Cookies are saved in your browser's sandboxed localStorage on your own device.
  • Transient Proxy Requests: When resolving restricted forum threads, cookie headers are forwarded to LPSG origin servers purely to authenticate media subrequests.
  • No Server Database Storage: Our Cloudflare Pages serverless functions do not store, log, or persist your cookie credentials to any permanent database.

4. Cloud Cache Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable the multi-device Cloud Cache Sync feature:

  • Thread metadata and direct video URLs are stored in your own Google Firebase Firestore project.
  • Data is organized under an isolated collection keyed to your sync identity.
  • You can click "Purge Cloud Cache" at any time to immediately delete all cached thread records from Firestore.

5. Device & Workspace Security

The workspace includes a dedicated password lock screen to secure the interface on shared or personal devices. Access tokens are encrypted and verified using cryptographic hashing algorithms (SHA-256).

6. Data Deletion & Control

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Clear Local Storage: Click "Sign Out / Disconnect" in the settings modal to instantly purge all stored LPSG cookies and session data from your browser.
  • Purge Cloud Cache: Click "Purge Cloud Cache" in the Cloud Cache Sync tab to delete all synchronized thread caches.
